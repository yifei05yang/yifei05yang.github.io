Undergraduate researcher · Expected graduation: June 2027

I am an undergraduate in Communication Engineering at Beijing Jiaotong University and Lancaster University. My research focuses on world models, visual navigation, and robot learning.

I am currently a research intern in Prof. Yan Wang's group at the Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University. I have also conducted research at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. I am interested in how robots can predict future states and use those predictions to plan and act in the physical world.
