I am an undergraduate in Communication Engineering at Beijing Jiaotong University and Lancaster University. My research focuses on world models, visual navigation, and robot learning.

I have conducted research at the Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University and The Chinese University of Hong Kong. I am interested in how robots can predict future states and use those predictions to plan and act in the physical world.
